# Route evidence and recent polymer literature

[Source dispositions](sources.json) distinguish reaction-class precedent,
structural analogues and directly relevant polymer literature. None of these
checks promotes an exact candidate or supplies a new computed property.

Hamilton et al. report CsF-assisted molecular PMDI N-alkylation
([DOI 10.1071/C97214](https://connectsci.au/ch/article-abstract/51/5/441/146332/N-Alkylation-of-Pyromellitic-Diimide-Solid-State)).
That supports the reaction class. The proposed DBU/MeCN reaction on crosslinked
beads still lacks the specific selectivity and core-retention evidence required
by the route gate. The separate dibenzyl-PMDI crystallographic paper is a
structural analogue; its preparative wording does not resolve that route.

Two closer literature connections are now included in the manuscript:

- [Oh Biying et al., ChemSusChem 2025](https://doi.org/10.1002/cssc.202500092)
  establish prior art for attaching phenothiazine/TEMPO groups to partially
  chloromethylated polystyrene. This is a lead for oxidation-active routes, not
  permission to transfer conditions or evidence to every linker/precursor. The
  publisher abstract and metadata were checked; supporting-file requests
  returned 403. Detailed conditions remain unverified in this pass.
- [Mohanty et al., Chemistry of Materials 2026](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.chemmater.6c01965)
  provide experimental/MD context for concentration-dependent kinetics in
  phthalimide polymers. The reviewed experiments use DMF electrolyte. This is
  not a matched MeCN interaction-energy benchmark or a validation of our finite
  neutral occupancy cycles.
