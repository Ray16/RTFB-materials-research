# JCIM rewrite and lead-validation plan

Target: full-length Article. Official author guidelines checked 1 November 2026:
https://researcher-resources.acs.org/publish/author_guidelines?coden=jcisd8
The guide was updated 8 October 2026. Its main Article instructions call for a
short title (preferably no more than twelve words), a concise three-to-four
sentence abstract, embedded figures, separate SI, a data-availability statement
and AI-use disclosure. ACS Fast Format permits an ordinary complete manuscript;
we do not apply the Letter or Application Note word limits to this Article.

Scientific question: which comparisons remain valid when a molecular redox
motif becomes a graft on a preformed polymer? Central contribution: a
context-preserving computational assessment that links exact product/precursor
identity, state-specific calculations, defined reaction conventions and matched
occupancy cycles. Hess's law, redox screening and chemical provenance are prior
art. Do not claim a new density functional, universal polymer optimum or new
thermodynamic identity. Contrast with Achazi's multistep screening and existing
polymer/transport studies, then show what the connected assessment adds.

Rewrite the main article from a fresh structure:
1. Introduction: decision problem, prior methods, missing link, research scope.
2. Computational framework and methods: exact records; permissible joins;
   composite energies/reference; reaction classes; occupancy differences;
   sampling and validation. Move audit/execution history to SI.
3. Results: independent accuracy baseline; route-admitted viologen and TEMPO;
   reaction-definition effects; PMDI molecular-to-polymer case; sequence versus
   spatial separation and sampling limits. Tie each result to a modeling choice.
4. Discussion: generalizable interpretation, applicability boundaries, lead
   priorities and what experimental validation remains necessary.
5. Conclusions: supported computational findings and reusable assessment logic.
6. SI description, Data and Software Availability, author statements and
   Acknowledgments with explicit AI drafting/code-assistance disclosure.

Retain the current immutable scientific snapshot. New charged-polymer runs are
not paper results until all physical gates and the independent reviewer pass.
Preserve the previous main text and SI under durable calcs/validation storage;
confirm that no Overleaf remote is connected before the edit pass. Build/check
both PDFs, inspect edited pages and prepare a compiled standalone export. Author
identity/funding/competing-interest confirmation and full archival deposition
remain required before submission.

Targeted workflow changes accompany this work: a command catalogue distinguishes
orchestration, stages, controls and recovery; property arithmetic retains matched
method/solvent/state/reference/QC declarations; shared context validation replaces
one duplicate-prone reaction assembly boundary; GPU gate/lock discovery follows
explicit configuration and runtime discovery. Existing frozen evidence remains
unchanged and old numerical functions retain explicitly unverified arithmetic
compatibility rather than acquiring new physical qualification automatically.

Lead priority: complete and independently validate the first charged-viologen
native component before expanding that resource class. Its earlier pilot was
stopped by storage protection. Check closed ownership and preserved records,
preflight a fresh source-bound recovery with unchanged physical gates, and
verify actual start. Only then schedule further eligible calculations under
ordinary reservations. Current molecular viologen/TEMPO and finite PMDI evidence
provide the scientific cases for the rewrite.
