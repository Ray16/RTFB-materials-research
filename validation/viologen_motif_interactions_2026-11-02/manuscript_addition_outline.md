# JCIM revision outline for the checked ionic controls

The research question is whether a favorable whole-chain neighboring-graft response supports an attractive local-motif interpretation. Four source frames now provide a controlled comparison at matched electronic and solvent models. Twelve A/B/AB triplets and 72 actual SCF frames support the answer: local capped interactions are repulsive in all four frames, whereas whole-chain E nonadditivity is negative in two. Cap-length sensitivity is small within the declared methyl-cap model, without proving chemical-cap or basis convergence.

The revision will explain the atom-level cut and charge states in Methods, show the four-frame comparison in Results, and retain the complete gas/MeCN and cap-length data in SI. It will state that the two operational quantities differ in linker/backbone/cap, deformation and continuum contributions and will not assign their difference uniquely. The new all-same one-spacer native G cycle will be reported separately from the electronic fragment result. The conclusion will use this quantitative counterexample to explain the assessment's scientific utility. No intrinsic DFT-accuracy or screening-algorithm superiority will be claimed.
