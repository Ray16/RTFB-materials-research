# Reviewed NDI radical minimum replacements

The methoxyethyl and n-butyl NDI radical anions had genuine negative modes at
preserved earlier geometries. Two bounded signed-mode searches were run for each.
The minus branch of each search passed all five strict optimizer criteria,
qualified native derivatives and active single-point energy checks; opposite
branches failed explicit convergence and remain excluded.

Each selected minimum then passed **157 independent checks, zero failures**:
exact graph, final optimizer geometry, all five convergence criteria, full native
spectrum, worst-contact finite-gradient replay, stationarity, active-energy units
and spin, and agreement between the primary and independent thermal readers.

- [Methoxyethyl NDI review](ndi_methoxyethyl/independent_review.json) and
  [promotion receipt](ndi_methoxyethyl/adoption_receipt.json).
- [n-Butyl NDI review](ndi_butyl/independent_review.json) and
  [promotion receipt](ndi_butyl/adoption_receipt.json).

Both replacements were installed under writer locks after verifying producer
controllers and numerical workers terminal. Complete original state directories
and their SHA-256 inventories are preserved in the paths recorded by each receipt.
Changed geometry invalidates old cross-point energies; no solution kinetic
validation is inferred. The first staging review failed because native policy had
not been selected in the private directory; those reports are retained as
`before_policy_selection_*`. Selecting the already-qualified native policy fixed
the staging omission; no physical threshold or numerical result was changed.

The generic repair helper was separately corrected to include projected thermal
partitions and validate a complete replacement before directory swaps, with
rollback and preserved histories. The full software suite passes 395 tests.
Raw Hessians and gradient arrays remain on shared storage, bound by the committed
source and review inventories. Exact-graft experimental accuracy is unvalidated.
