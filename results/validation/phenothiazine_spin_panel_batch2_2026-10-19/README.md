# Source-bound spin batch

Two first-ready states of DMeOMEEPT, the fourth experimental catholyte parent.

`plan.json` freezes the selected identities and producer files. `launch.json` and
`start_verification.json` distinguish submitted controllers from observed resident
GPU calculations, with disjoint two-CPU sets and two native threads. The workers
reuse the bounded source-addressed spin validator and the corrected complete-cache
repair transaction. Pending source states hold no GPU. No physical gate changes.

Progress is under `parents/<id>/status.json`; case results point to the preserved
raw helpers and detailed reports. Readiness, near-spin ensembles, promotion waits
and experimental accuracy remain separate. Do not edit plan-bound numerical
producers while these workers are active. These controls qualify exact parents,
not grafted structures.
