The existing `tempo` local graft corresponds to 4-hydroxy-TEMPO attached through
its 4-oxygen to the modeled benzyl carbon. Its precursor matches PubChem CID
137994. [US6441243B1, Example A4](https://patents.google.com/patent/US6441243B1/en)
reports this attachment chemistry on preformed, 1%-divinylbenzene Merrifield
resin using NaH in DMF or THF. The patent reports 0.9 mmol of N-oxyl per gram of
the washed product. This is an exact-support precedent for the connection, with
the local 4-methylbenzyl graph used here as a model of the polymer site.

The prospective route gate gives **proceed** for further redox evaluation. The
overall sodium-hydride bookkeeping conserves atoms, charge and the nitroxide
radical count. The patent's reported loading and starting chloride density use
different mass bases; they are not a conversion for our material. One source's
reported selectivity and radical retention are not independent replication or
evidence of electrochemical cycling. The actual-condition reaction free energy,
ion speciation, kinetics and bead accessibility remain unresolved.

The existing exact local graft's one-electron oxidation potential was
independently reconstructed from the active solution energies and four native
Hessian partitions, including the matched Fc reference. It is **+0.579 V versus
Fc/Fc⁺** at the 50 cm⁻¹ rotor cutoff in the MeCN model; the 25 and 100 cm⁻¹
values are +0.576 and +0.579 V. This is an unfitted calculation, not an
experimental potential for the graft or a prediction interval. The benchmark's
unsubstituted TEMPO parent is a different molecule and does not validate this
exact graft. Reference, basis and conformer limitations still apply.

Applying the shared conjunctive policy gives **material decision: pending**.
The attachment-route axis passes; redox-window acceptance, capacity, state
stability, charge transfer, grafting thermodynamics, motif association,
neighbor coupling, occupancy sensitivity and accessibility remain pending.
No new quantum job was launched, and this separate assessment does not silently
change the previously frozen 879-graph decision ledger or create a synthesis
shortlist. The prior route registry was archived before the append; existing
entries and historical evidence are reconciled in the registry audit.
