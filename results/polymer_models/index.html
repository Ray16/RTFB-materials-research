These are method controls, not synthesis-shortlist admissions. Prepared and xTB views are previews. Electronic ΔE is distinct from interaction ΔG and does not establish an equilibrium stacking population or poor polymer performance.

Design objective: weak interaction between redox sites and polymer segments. Strong persistent pi stacking and undesired intersite electron transfer are risks. More negative association energies are concerns to investigate, not benefits to maximize. This neutral pilot has not computed electron-transfer coupling or rates. It covers monomers, dimers, trimers and separate-segment pairs; no tetramers.
