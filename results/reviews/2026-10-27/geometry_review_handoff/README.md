# Geometry-review handoff to electronic consumers

The [handoff audit](summary.json) records thirteen byte-identical independent
geometry reviews published at the paths declared by the occupied site-2
hybrid-energy batches. Those geometries had finished after the original observer
window and their later reviews were stored in a separate review directory.
The consumer therefore continued to report `upstream_pending`.

Each source review was matched to the exact geometry campaign, its full backing
hashes and its geometry-only qualification scope. Existing consumer files were
never replaced. No energy or native free energy was supplied by this handoff.
Four tests cover exact-byte/idempotent publication, wrong identities, changed
existing files and inappropriate qualification scope.

The [independent-review snapshot](current_review_snapshot.json) records the
broader expansion's current reviews. Failed source attempts remain preserved.
It does not establish conformer convergence or a finished polymer ensemble.
