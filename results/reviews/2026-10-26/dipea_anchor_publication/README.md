Cook et al. report an independent NMR pKa estimate of 18.7 in MeCN for a
DIPEAH hexafluorophosphate salt. The older 18.6 value remains a distinct PCM
estimate. The manuscript and slide 12 now state the experimental salt identity
and retain the unmatched counterion, activity and temperature qualifications.
The conditional relative DIPEA/DBU transfer and DIPEA/HCl capture comparisons
are independently reconstructed; neither a measured grafting free energy nor
a correction to the complete route is inferred. No candidate decision or
primary reaction energy changed.

The new evidence epoch copies the previous frozen numerical/figure files and
adds the primary-source assessment, producer and three-test record. The earlier
epoch is unchanged. Tests cover source identity, numerical signs, missing values,
corrupted or promoted hybrid claims, and safe evidence extension. The article
and SI were rebuilt, rendered, visually reviewed separately from compiler checks,
and exported to a self-contained ZIP whose document text matches the checked
PDFs. No Overleaf project has been connected or created.

Source XML is under `calcs/literature/2026-10-26_dipea_experiment/` with its
CC BY-NC 3.0 license; it is not silently relicensed as project code. Compiler
logs and rendered review pages remain under `calcs/validation/`. Broader
conformer ensembles, actual graft selectivity, material performance and public
data deposition remain open.
