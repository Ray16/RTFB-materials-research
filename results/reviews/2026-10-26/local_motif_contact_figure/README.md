# Local motif contact figure

[Figure PDF](../../../figures/polymer_models/local_motif_contacts.pdf) · [PNG](../../../figures/polymer_models/local_motif_contacts.png) · [Plot data and hashes](../../../figures/polymer_models/local_motif_contacts.json) · [Checks](summary.json) · [Tests](tests.xml)

The four points are frozen phthalimide capped-fragment poses from the independently checked local panel. Circles and squares distinguish adjacent and one-spacer graft sites. The plotted energy is `E(AB) − E(A) − E(B)` in the acetonitrile model at the central cap length; error bars span the three tested cap lengths and are not uncertainty intervals. The two close poses are approximately −24.8 and −26.2 kJ/mol; the separated poses are near zero.

The values include cap and continuum response and are uncorrected for basis-set superposition. Four poses do not establish populations, a general spacing trend, an association free energy or electron-transfer coupling. No new quantum calculations were run. The [plotting script](../../../../scripts/plotting/polymer_models/plot_local_motif_contacts.py) verifies the source hashes and independently reconstructs each interaction before plotting.
