# CPU counterpoise: one-spacer phthalimide control

The independently reviewed [geometry](../counterpoise_control/prepared_one_spacer_cpu_geometry.json) defines a frozen 38-atom capped-fragment pair. The A-own SCF has converged, its checkpoint passed independent review, and its [orbital-gradient check](own_stationarity_ghost_launch.json) passed the declared threshold. The A-ghost resource pilot is running; its [live status](../../../../calcs/local_motif_counterpoise/cpu_one_spacer_ghost_2026-10-26/status.json) remains unqualified.

The [gated workflow](remaining_preparation.json) proceeds to AB, B-own and B-ghost only after both A terms pass their checkpoint and orbital-stationarity checks. It then requires independent checks for each remaining term before assembling the five-term electronic counterpoise sum. The result will be **gas phase for a frozen capped-fragment control**; it will not be a MeCN correction, an association free energy, electronic coupling or a converged polymer ensemble.

The sampled host-RSS guard is 32 GiB per worker; sampling can detect a transient allocation only after it occurs. The larger ghost basis has 1,026 functions versus 513 for A-own, so the small-system control does not establish its memory requirement. Checkpoints and the original failed attempts are preserved on shared storage.

The exact CPU procedures were checked against source-bound water-dimer controls: [checkpoint restart](restart_check.json), [A-own stationarity](stationarity_water_check.json), [ghost stationarity](ghost_stationarity_water_check_v2.json), and [the three remaining terms](remaining_water_check.json). The [initial failed restart](initial_restart_diagnostic.json) and subsequent corrections are retained. [Workflow tests](complete_workflow_tests_final.xml) cover missing terms, identity mismatches, resource scheduling and counterpoise arithmetic.
