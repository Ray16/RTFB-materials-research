# Independent PMDI nuclear replay and manuscript integration

The [current raw-state package](../../../releases/neighbor_cycle/README.md)
reproduces the complete finite PMDI cycle after archive extraction and relocation.
The standard-library mode reconstructs fourteen cached states and fourteen balanced comparisons;
the separately locked NumPy mode reconstructs thirteen molecular Hessian mode
sets, nuclear sums at three rotor cutoffs and monatomic chloride translation.
Both isolated replays block original repository reads. The old arithmetic-only
archive and its validation remain preserved in the release history.

The [test record](tests.xml) contains 93 passing manuscript-claim and raw-replay
checks. The fourteen raw-state integration checks include six changes that pass
cached arithmetic but fail independent nuclear reconstruction: modified or
borrowed Hessians, altered molecular or atomic masses, changed symmetry and
changed frequencies. No original quantum calculation is repeated by this replay.

The [new immutable evidence epoch](../../../../manuscript/evidence/2026-10-26_native_replay/manifest.json)
binds the archive identities, extracted replay, negative checks and producer
sources. The [main paper](../../../../manuscript/manuscript.pdf) reports the
addendum in Data and Code Availability; the [SI](../../../../manuscript/supporting_information.pdf)
describes the numerical scope. Both remain 18 pages with no overfull boxes or
unresolved references. [Rendered review](visual_review.json) covers all pages
via contact sheets and the revised prose at full resolution. All nineteen
scientific figure PDFs match the previous snapshot exactly.

The [self-contained ZIP](../../../../manuscript/overleaf_upload_2026-10-26_native_replay.zip)
builds with text identical to the checked documents. [Export proof](export.json)
does not claim an Overleaf connection or upload. Author declarations, final data
licensing and public DOI deposition remain open.

The [live native-pilot observation](native_progress.json) records a completed
first coupled-response solve with its original-equation residual check. This
is an unqualified intermediate, not a finished Hessian or free energy. Broader
conformer convergence, candidate-specific material evidence and the deferred
repository consolidation remain open.
