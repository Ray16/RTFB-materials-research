# Candidate exploration

Return to the [results index](../README.md). Use the [candidate workbook](../candidates/latest.xlsx)
and [comparison browser](../candidates/figures/index.html) for current computed
properties. The records here explain how structures were selected; enumeration
and source curation do not imply completed calculations.

Open the [integrated design/evaluation report](expansion/index.html) for systematic
coverage, declared controls, matched effects and candidate-level review actions.
The [workflow guide](../../docs/CANDIDATE_EXPANSION_LOOP.md) explains how existing
evaluation outputs feed the next design question. The report preserves the source
feed's date; collecting a new view is not a new quantum calculation or route approval.

Exact candidate identities and the active export snapshot are recorded in
[current.json](../candidates/current.json). Source-parent measurements retain their
original solvent/reference and are not assigned to an exact graft. Bulk source
files remain under `data/raw/discovery_sources/` on shared storage, with source
URLs and hashes in their curation manifests. Campaign-local preparation and
execution records stay in their original dated directories.
