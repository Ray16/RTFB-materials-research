\section{Benchmark intervals and post hoc reference diagnostics}
The complete panel has 39 endpoints from 30 molecules and seven studies. Molecule-cluster 95\% percentile bootstrap intervals are [0.213,0.286] V for MAE, [0.245,0.313] V for RMSE and [0.128,0.257] V for bias. Study-cluster intervals are [0.178,0.306], [0.211,0.325] and [0.053,0.285] V, respectively. Both use 2,000 replicates and seed 20261021. The seven study clusters have unequal sizes; their percentile intervals are weakly calibrated. Molecule resampling retains common study and family effects and is also not a prediction interval for a new molecule.

\subsection{Directional electronic solution relaxation of the exact TEMPO graft}
The oxidized $O$ state has charge $+1$ and singlet multiplicity; the reduced $R$ state has charge zero and doublet multiplicity. Each uses its previously checked r$^2$SCAN-D4/def2-SVP/SMD minimum. The shared heavy-atom ordering and full hydrogen connectivity match the declared graft. Their heavy-atom RMSD is 0.137 \AA; the three bridge-torsion differences are 10.84, 2.24 and 1.88 degrees. The declared 0.3 \AA\ and 30-degree diagnostics identify a paired local basin; they do not establish a conformer ensemble.

All six fresh calculations use $\omega$B97M-V/def2-TZVPD with density fitting and native MeCN SMD. The native solvent constants are $\epsilon_s=35.688$ and $\epsilon_{\rm op}=1.80687364$. Optical polarization relaxes self-consistently during each vertical charge change while the initial slow polarization is frozen using a Pekar partition.\cite{you2015nonequilibrium} Each vertical event retains the same coordinates, basis and cavity for both electronic states. The native geometry-only CDS functional is included once; its treatment away from equilibrium is a stated model assumption, not a solvent-dynamics validation. With $Q_X$ denoting the geometry of state $X$ and $D_X$ its equilibrium density,
\begin{align}
 L_{O\to R}&=E_R^{\rm neq}(Q_O;D_O)-E_R^{\rm eq}(Q_R),\\
 L_{R\to O}&=E_O^{\rm neq}(Q_R;D_R)-E_O^{\rm eq}(Q_O).
\end{align}
The first direction separates into $E_R^{\rm eq}(Q_O)-E_R^{\rm eq}(Q_R)$ and $E_R^{\rm neq}(Q_O;D_O)-E_R^{\rm eq}(Q_O)$, with the analogous reverse expressions. The first component includes equilibrium solvation and geometry response; it is not an isolated gas inner-sphere term. No gas-inner or Born-outer quantity is added to either total. These electronic-structure energies include no nuclear thermal correction, explicit counterion or polymer environment.

\subsection{Charged viologen occupancy construction and salt convention}
The same precursor/attachment identity defines a conditional five-repeat viologen panel with reactive benzyl sites 2, 3 and 4 and plain-styrene padding at sites 1 and 5. Each of two declared backbone configurations has an empty graph, three singly grafted graphs and adjacent (2,3) or one-spacer (2,4) double grafts. The four corresponding nonadditivity cycles conserve atoms and charge. Independent examination of the initial XYZ structures verifies the ten-carbon, nine-bond open backbone, stereochemistry, benzyl--pyridinium nitrogen attachments and residual benzyl chlorides. Figure~\ref{fig:viologen-occupancy-construction} shows the actual two-graft graphs; it does not represent a spatial arrangement or an interaction energy.

ETKDGv3 requests four starting conformers per graph, using seed 20261031 plus the graph index and at most 1,000 embedding iterations. A failed embedding triggers one declared random-coordinate retry with the same seed. Connectivity-valid structures are ranked after 200 MMFF steps; this pre-relaxation did not converge and supplies initial coordinates only. Neither these force-field energies nor the starting contact distances enter solution thermodynamics. Global charge/spin hypotheses are recorded separately from graph construction, without assigning charge localization or spin ordering. No charged-polymer energy, conformer ensemble or native nuclear partition is inferred from this preparation.

A subsequent fixed-geometry solver control evaluates the 137-atom, fully oxidized two-graft model as a charge-$+4$ singlet in MeCN with omitted separated spectator ions. \section{Relative-basicity controls}
The balanced reactions $\mathrm{B+DBUH^+\rightarrow BH^++DBU}$ avoid HCl, chloride and a free-proton reference. This two-reaction control does not validate DIPEA, contact pairs or the complete grafting reaction and cannot assign the DBU/HCl discrepancy to a unique species. The separated model uses bare chloride in a continuum. Recent MeCN ion-solvation comparisons identify both species-dependent errors and sensitivity to absolute single-ion conventions, so the capture discrepancy cannot be assigned to chloride alone.\cite{pliego2024} Acid--chloride homoconjugation, such as $\mathrm{HCl+Cl^-\rightleftharpoons HCl_2^-}$, would introduce an additional species and acid-dependent speciation. It is not included in either ionic representation; no population or homoconjugation constant is inferred from the free-ion pKa comparison.
Spectrophotometric titration against bromocresol green reports $\log K^{\mathrm H}=18.64(1)$ for DIPEA in MeCN at $(25.0\pm0.1)$ $^\circ$C.\cite{barisic2021dipea} A separate NMR competition study estimates pKa 18.7 for diisopropylethylammonium hexafluorophosphate in MeCN-d$_3$.\cite{cook2024dipea} The latter uses a 0.02 M initial salt solution and triethylamine reference; its supporting table labels negative values as a $K_a$ ratio and applies an absolute value in the reported logarithm. We retain the authors' stated estimate rather than independently refitting its sign convention. The two sources are not pooled into an uncertainty interval. With pKa(HCl)=10.30, the 18.64 value instead implies $-47.60$ \kj\ for the separated capture term, compared with $-70.98$ \kj\ computed.
