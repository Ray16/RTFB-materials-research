# Follow-up to the language-edit clarification notes

Both definition issues raised during the language edit are addressed in the
current revision; the earlier notes remain in Git history.

- Methods and the calculation-level table now distinguish ionic viologen
  r²SCAN-D4 electronic/Hessian calculations from corrected occupied-phthalimide
  sampling with ωB97M-V electronic energies and r²SCAN-D4 Hessians. The
  clarification was checked against 12 frozen ionic records and 31 qualified
  native-sampling records (including one empty-backbone component), including their source hashes.
- The main text and SI define the computed Fc reference as a composite solution
  Gibbs free-energy difference and identify the cited photoemission quantity as
  an adiabatic ionisation energy. A matched thermodynamic conversion is not
  claimed, and neither a reference replacement nor a calibrated Fc error is
  inferred from their difference.

See method_clarification.json for the source bindings. Display equations and
frozen numerical inputs are unchanged. Author information and public-release
completion remain pending.
