\section{Equations used}
\textbf{Redox potential.} For a one-electron couple O + e⁻ → R with free energies G = $E_{\mathrm{SMD}}$ + $G_{\mathrm{thermal}}$,

\[
  E_{\mathrm{vs\,Fc}} = -\frac{G(\mathrm{R}) - G(\mathrm{O})}{e} - E_{\mathrm{abs}}(\mathrm{Fc}),\qquad E_{\mathrm{abs}}(\mathrm{Fc}) = G(\mathrm{Fc}^{+}) - G(\mathrm{Fc}),
\]
with ferrocene computed at the same level and with the same thermal tier as the molecule. Constants at 298.15 K: RT/F = 0.025693 V; Nernst slope 0.059159 V per decade. A two-electron candidate's reported potential is the mean of its two couples.

\textbf{Capacity.} C = nF/(3.6 M) in mAh g⁻¹, with n the number of usable electrons (those whose couples lie in the window [−3.0, +2.0] V vs Fc on the candidate's side of the 0 V divider) and M the neutral polystyrene repeat-unit mass: the capped-graft mass plus one carbon (12.011 g mol⁻¹), because the cap's CH₃–Ar becomes –[CH₂–CH(Ar)]– in the polymer. The salt-loaded variant adds n counterions to M (PF₆⁻, 144.96 g mol⁻¹, for catholytes; TBA⁺, 242.47 g mol⁻¹, for anolytes).

\textbf{Outer-sphere reorganization (Born estimate).} For a sphere of radius a,

\[
  \lambda_{\mathrm{o}} = \frac{z^{2} e^{2}}{8\pi\varepsilon_{0} a}\left(\frac{1}{\varepsilon_{\mathrm{op}}} - \frac{1}{\varepsilon_{\mathrm{s}}}\right),
\]
with $\varepsilon_{\mathrm{s}}$ = 37.5, $\varepsilon_{\mathrm{op}}$ = $n_{\mathrm{D}}^{2}$ = 1.806 ($n_{\mathrm{D}}$ = 1.344), z = 1, and a the radius of the sphere with the same solvent-accessible surface area as the molecule. The molecular-cavity value uses the same slow-polarization (Pekar) factor with the IEF-PCM response operators of the real cavity, applied to the change in surface potential between the two charge states; it reproduces the Born value exactly for a single-atom cavity (code check \texttt{lambda\_\allowbreak{}outer.\allowbreak{}sphere\_\allowbreak{}sanity\_\allowbreak{}check}).

\textbf{Inner-sphere reorganization (four-point).}

\[
  \lambda_{\mathrm{i}} = \bigl[E_{\mathrm{O}}(\text{geometry of R}) - E_{\mathrm{O}}(\text{geometry of O})\bigr] + \bigl[E_{\mathrm{R}}(\text{geometry of O}) - E_{\mathrm{R}}(\text{geometry of R})\bigr].
\]
The values in this work use gas-phase energies at the SMD geometries (main text).

\textbf{Disproportionation.} $\Delta G_{\mathrm{disp}}$ = $G(\mathrm{R}^{q+1}) + G(\mathrm{R}^{q-1}) - 2G(\mathrm{R}^{q})$, equal to F times the spacing between the two waves.

\textbf{Thermal corrections.} Pool tier: GFN2-xTB Hessian at the DFT geometry, xTB thermodynamics at 298.15 K. DFT-frequency tier: r²SCAN-D4/def2-SVP(D) SMD Hessian with grid response; translations and rotations are projected out and the internal modes are passed to the xTB thermodynamics routine with the quasi-RRHO treatment of low modes (rotor cutoff 50 cm⁻¹) [Grimme 2012], plus a spin-degeneracy term.
