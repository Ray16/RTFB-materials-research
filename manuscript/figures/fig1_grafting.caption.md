# Figure 1 — the grafted-motif abstraction

**File:** `fig1_grafting.png` (300 dpi; molecule depictions are high-resolution
RDKit raster embedded in the layout). PNG only, per the figure style contract.

**Figure 1. A redox motif is modeled as an exact capped graft, with redox states
defined per motif.**
**(a) Grafting abstraction.** Each candidate is built by attaching a redox motif
(here 5,10-dihydrophenazine, DMPZ, the p-type catholyte scaffold used in the
showcase; both ring nitrogens are N–H in the free motif) to a *4-methylbenzyl* cap
that serves as a Merrifield (poly(vinylbenzyl)) backbone proxy; the ring methyl
*para* to the benzylic linker stands in for the polymer chain. Grafting is a
balanced local substitution onto 4-methylbenzyl chloride (`Cc1ccc(CCl)cc1`),
releasing HCl. **Only one nitrogen (N5) carries the Merrifield backbone graft**
(highlighted orange: the new benzylic C–N bond); **the other nitrogen (N10)
carries a small ethyl *tuning* cap, not a second graft.** The depicted capped
graft, *N*-(4-methylbenzyl)-*N*′-ethyl-DMPZ
(`CCN1c2ccccc2N(Cc2ccc(C)cc2)c2ccccc21`), is byte-identical to the registered
model (`config/custom_candidates.py`, id `exp_dmpz_mb_ethyl`). **(b) Per-motif
redox states.** The ladder is defined on the capped graft as adjacent
one-electron couples from the resting state; for this p-type motif, neutral →
ox1 (+1, doublet radical cation) → ox2 (+2, singlet dication), reported vs Fc/Fc⁺.
Because **both nitrogens are substituted (N,N′-disubstituted), N–H deprotonation on
oxidation is blocked, giving a reversible 2e⁻ couple** — the design basis for using
DMPZ as a 2-electron catholyte. (n-type anolyte motifs instead reduce; ambipolar
motifs carry couples on both sides.)

**Interpretation / caveats.**
- **A capped graft, a soluble parent, and an oligomer are three distinct models**
  (banner): the capped graft fixes local electronic structure and attachment
  chemistry but does not represent chain packing, polymer solubility, or
  cooperative effects, and inherits no measured property from any soluble parent.
- The badges ("0", "+1 •", "2+") are **formal overall charge/spin labels on a
  constant depiction**, not QM spin-density or bond-order predictions. Elemental
  composition and connectivity are held fixed across the ladder; each computed
  state resolves its own (charge, multiplicity) at the DFT level in the pipeline.

**Provenance.** Cap scaffold and motif fragment taken verbatim from
`config/custom_candidates.py`; structures depicted with RDKit. Generated by
