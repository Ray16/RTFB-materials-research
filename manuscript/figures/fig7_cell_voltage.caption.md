**Figure 7. Proposed non-aqueous full cells read off a shared ferrocene scale (computed proposals, not demonstrated devices).**
All potentials share the ferrocene (Fc/Fc⁺) scale in TBAPF₆/MeCN, so a nominal cell voltage is *E*₍cell₎ = *E*(catholyte) − *E*(anolyte). The upper green band is the catholyte pool (p-type): the grafted 5,10-dihydrophenazines (DMPZ) and one 3,7-dimethoxyphenothiazine (PTZ), spanning +0.21 to +0.78 V, with the viable DMPZ window near +0.3 to +0.6 V. The lower blue band is the anolyte pool (n-type quinone/imide/pyridine; 85 candidates, including the two single-wave mono-imide controls, shown as a rug), spanning −1.63 to −0.63 V. Three of the four pairings in Table 3 are drawn as vertical arrows annotated with the resulting cell voltage: N′-ethyl DMPZ (+0.43 V) × alkoxy-anthraquinone [2-(4-methylbenzyloxy)-6-methoxyanthracene-9,10-dione, −1.49 V] → **1.91 V**; N′-ethyl DMPZ (+0.43 V) × ether-tethered pyromellitic diimide (PMDI, −1.54 V) → **1.97 V**; and the more electron-poor N′-(2,2,2-trifluoroethyl) DMPZ (+0.62 V) × the same ether-PMDI (−1.54 V) → **2.17 V** (marked with a star), extending the proposal beyond 2.1 V. These are computed values. Subtracting each family's measured bias at the pool's thermal tier (DMPZ +0.30 V from the BMEPZ check; quinones +0.24 V and imides +0.05 V from the benchmark) gives 1.85, 1.72 and 1.92 V; the PMDI pairs drop most because the imide bias is small. Potentials are the mean of the two one-electron couples from the scored pool (Table S7; xTB thermal tier). **These are computed proposals from two computed half-cell potentials, not demonstrated cells:** attainable capacity, cycling, crossover, charged-state stability and synthesis of the exact grafted structures are untested, and a capped graft is not its soluble parent or an oligomer.
